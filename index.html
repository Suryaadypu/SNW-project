Personal Security

Passwords

make them long-ish. mix symbols n all that. try not to reuse (yea it's annoying).

Two-Factor Auth

2FA is kinda annoying but saves you from headaches later.

Updates

keep apps updated. most patches fix stuff ppl usually ignore.

Public Wi-Fi

don’t do banking on café wifi lol. vpn helps a bit.

Backups

save your files somewhere. cloud, hard drive, whatever. just do backups.

Links

hover first. if it looks weird, skip it. simple.

Business Security

Train the team

make sure folks know basic phishing stuff. one wrong click hurts.

Firewalls

just keep them on + configured (pls don't overthink).

Access limits

give ppl only what they need. cleaner + safer.

Regular checks

maybe check passwords/settings sometimes — ppl forget.

Encrypt

encrypt important files, esp customer stuff.

Plan

have a “what if things break” plan. saves panic later.

Mobile

Lock it

pin, pattern, fingerprint — anything is better than nothing.

Official stores

just use play store or app store. shady sites = nope.

Permissions

calculator doesn’t need your contacts lol. remove weird perms.

Updates

install updates when your phone nags you.

Bluetooth

turn off when not needed. small thing but helps.

Tracking

turn on find‑my‑device or whatever your phone has.

Email

Phishing

check sender, weird spelling, sketch links. delete if unsure.

Attachments

don’t open random attachments. even if it looks "important".

Verify things

call the company if an email feels sus. better safe.

Encrypt

use encryption for sensitive messages (if you know how lol).

No passwords

pls never send passwords in email. password managers exist.

2FA

turn on 2FA for email. if someone gets your mail, everything falls apart.

Social

Privacy

set posts to friends-only. strangers don’t need your life story.

Sharing

don’t overshare personal stuff. internet never forgets.

Friends

ignore random friend reqs. half of them are bots anyway.

Links

even if a friend sends a link, double check. hacked accounts send spam.

Passwords

strong + unique passwords per account. yes, really.

Apps

remove old apps connected to your social accounts. you prob forgot some.

thx for reading :) stay safe
